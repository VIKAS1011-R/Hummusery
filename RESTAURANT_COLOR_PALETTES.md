## 🎨 Color Psychology for Restaurants

### Colors That Stimulate Appetite:

- **Red**: Increases urgency, stimulates appetite
- **Orange**: Creates warmth, encourages social interaction
- **Yellow**: Promotes happiness, speeds up decision making
- **Brown**: Suggests natural, organic, comfort

### Colors That Build Trust:

- **Blue**: Reliability, cleanliness (use sparingly in food contexts)
- **Green**: Health, freshness, natural
- **White**: Cleanliness, simplicity
- **Gray**: Sophistication, neutrality

### Colors to Avoid:

- **Purple**: Can suppress appetite
- **Bright Blue**: May make food look unappetizing
- **Black**: Can feel too heavy (use as accent only)

## 🎯 Implementation Guidelines

### Light Theme Colors:

- **Background**: Pure white (#ffffff)
- **Surface**: Light gray (#f8fafc)
- **Text Primary**: Dark gray (#111827)
- **Text Secondary**: Medium gray (#6b7280)
- **Borders**: Light gray (#e5e7eb)

### Dark Theme Colors:

- **Background**: Very dark gray (#0f172a)
- **Surface**: Dark gray (#1e293b)
- **Text Primary**: Light gray (#f1f5f9)
- **Text Secondary**: Medium gray (#94a3b8)
- **Borders**: Dark gray (#334155)
